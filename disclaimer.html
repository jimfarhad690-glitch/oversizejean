2. Indigo Bleeding & Natural Crocking Disclaimer

CRITICAL FABRIC NOTICE: GENUINE ROPE-DYED INDIGO

Our raw Japanese selvedge denim (including the Kuroki Mill Raw Indigo Carpenter and Atelier Pleated styles) is saturated in pure, unadulterated indigo dye. Dry and wet crocking (color transfer) is an inherent property of genuine raw denim, not a material defect.

By purchasing raw indigo denim from oversizejean.com, you explicitly acknowledge and accept that:

  • Footwear Contact: Indigo pigment will inevitably transfer onto light-colored sneakers, laces, and socks during initial wears. We strongly advise pairing raw selvedge with dark sneakers or boots during the break-in period.
  • Furniture & Seating: Unwashed indigo denim may transfer onto light-colored leather car seats, upholstered furniture, white couches, or pale canvas bags.
  • First Soak: A cold water soak with a tablespoon of white vinegar can help set the outer indigo dye layer, though natural bleeding will still occur as fades develop.

3. Sizing, Drape & Shuttle Loom Tolerances

Our garments are woven on mid-century vintage shuttle looms (e.g. Toyoda G9 models) running at authentic low tensions. This vintage weaving technique produces organic slub variations that give our oversized denim its signature structure and drape.

As a consequence of shrinkage dynamics and artisan tailoring:

  • Measurement Tolerance: Leg openings, waist circumferences, inseam lengths, and front rise measurements are subject to a normal industry tolerance of ±0.5 inches (1.27 cm).
  • Wash Shrinkage: While all raw denim styles are sanforized (pre-shrunk), minor residual shrinkage of approximately 1% to 2% in the inseam may occur after the first cold wash and line dry. Hot water washing or mechanical machine tumble drying will cause non-standard shrinkage and void warranty protections.
  • Drape Variance: Visual stacking and puddle effects depend significantly on customer height, leg-to-torso proportions, and footwear outsole height. The Baggy Fit Calculator provides calculated recommendations based on statistical norms, but individual anatomy may yield unique stacking behaviors.

4. Hand-Finished Wash & Distressing Variations

Each pair of our 1996 Super-Wide Vintage Stone Wash and Arctic Bleach Acid Stacking Jeans undergoes individual hand-distressing, pumice stone tumbling, whisker scraping, and hand-applied mineral tinting.

Consequently, no two pairs are visually identical. Fray patterns at the hem, whisker placements at the lap, and stone wash gradient nuances will vary subtly from garment to garment, making each piece an individual archive artifact. These variations are celebrated characteristics of high-fashion streetwear and do not constitute flaws.

5. Hardware & Metal Rivet Notice

oversizejean.com garments utilize solid hand-hammered copper rivets, custom brass donut buttons, and gunmetal finishes. Individuals with documented contact allergies to nickel, copper, or brass should exercise caution. All buttons and rivets meet international safety guidelines, but direct skin contact against unbelted waistbands may cause localized sensitivity in hyper-reactive individuals.

6. Digital Display & Color Accuracy

We have made every possible effort to photograph and calibrate our denim under high-CRI 5600K daylight-balanced studio conditions. However, the exact tone, saturation, and contrast rendered on your viewing display depends upon your individual screen hardware, calibration profile (e.g., sRGB, DCI-P3, Apple TrueTone), and ambient lighting. We cannot guarantee that your monitor's display of any wash color will be 100% physically identical to the real garment in natural sunlight.
