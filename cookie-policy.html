1. What Are Cookies and Storage Technologies?

Cookies and HTML5 web storage are small data files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit oversizejean.com. They are widely used by modern web applications to remember your preferences and ensure shopping bag continuity.

2. Cookies We Deploy

We strictly limit cookie usage to essential and functional operations:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Required to enable checkout security, CSRF protection, and fraud prevention.
  • Performance Cookies: Aggregated anonymous latency logs to identify site slowdowns and optimize page speed.
  • Zero Advertising Cookies: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels.

3. Local Storage (`localStorage`) Usage

oversizejean.com utilizes HTML5 `localStorage` for client-side state management:

  • osj_cart: Stores the items, sizes, quantities, and cuts added to your shopping bag.
  • osj_wishlist: Stores the product IDs marked as favorites with the heart icon.

This data remains solely inside your browser and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser cache or clicking "Clear Bag" in the shopping bag drawer.

4. Third-Party Technologies

When you execute a simulated or live checkout, secure payment processors (such as Stripe or Apple Pay) may place specialized fraud-detection cookies to authenticate cardholder identity.

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can modify your browser settings to block or delete cookies. If you disable cookies, note that core features such as the shopping bag and Baggy Fit Calculator will continue functioning via client-side storage, but checkout session continuity may be affected.
