2. Dispatch Processing & Tracking

All orders placed Monday through Friday prior to 14:00 JST are processed and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours. Once your denim leaves our hub, you will automatically receive an email and SMS containing your live tracking number with door-to-door GPS milestones.

3. Customs Duties & Import Taxes

For orders shipped to the United States, Japan, and the United Kingdom (under £135), all duties and import sales taxes are included at checkout with zero unexpected fees on delivery.

For other international jurisdictions, local customs duties and VAT may be assessed by your local border agency. oversizejean.com is not responsible for localized customs clearance delays.

4. The 30-Day Baggy Guarantee (Free Exchanges)

WORRY-FREE SILHOUETTE TESTING

We know finding your ideal volume—between subtle relaxed, 90s skater, and floor puddle—takes experimentation. We provide 100% free size and silhouette exchanges for 30 calendar days following delivery.

If your pair feels too long, too wide, or if you wish to swap from Cut 01 (Super Wide) to Cut 02 (Balloon Barrel), our returns concierge will issue a complimentary pre-paid return shipping label and reserve your replacement pair instantly.

5. Return Eligibility & State

To maintain fairness and hygiene for our streetwear community, returned garments must satisfy the following conditions:

  • Unwashed & Unaltered: Jeans must not have been soaked, machine-washed, starched, or hemmed.
  • Unworn: Garments may be tried on indoors for fit checking only. Jeans showing outdoor scuffs, heel drag tear, or pet hair will be returned to the sender.
  • Original Packaging & Tags: All original Japanese selvedge hangtags, flasher paper, and canvas storage pouches must be enclosed.

6. How to Start a Return or Exchange

Follow these three simple steps:

  1. Navigate to our digital portal or email returns@oversizejean.com with your order number (e.g., #OSJ-123456).
  2. Select whether you prefer a direct size exchange, silhouette switch, store credit (with +10% bonus credit), or full refund to your original payment method.
  3. Print the generated DHL/FedEx prepaid return label, affix it to the original reusable mailer, and drop it off at any authorized courier depot.

7. Refund Processing & Restocking Fees

We charge $0 restocking fees. Once our Okayama quality inspection team receives your return and verifies unworn condition (typically within 48 hours of depot arrival), your refund will be released back to your original payment method. Depending on your banking institution, funds appear on your statement within 3 to 5 business days.

8. Damaged, Defective or Transit Issues

In the unlikely event of transit box damage or hardware defect (e.g. sheared rivet), please notify us within 7 days of package delivery with clear photographs. We will immediately dispatch an expedited replacement pair at zero additional cost to you.
