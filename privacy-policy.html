1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

At oversizejean.com, your privacy is fundamental to our craft. We believe in minimal data footprint: we only collect the absolute minimum information required to deliver your denim, calculate custom fit recommendations, process payments securely, and protect against fraudulent activity.

This policy applies to all visitors, buyers, and community members globally accessing our services.

2. Information We Collect

We categorize personal data into three distinct tiers:

  • Directly Provided Order Data: Name, shipping address, billing address, email address, phone number, and selected waist and inseam sizing.
  • Baggy Fit Calculator Data: Height, weight, and preferred drape profile entered into our sizing tool. Note: Calculator inputs are processed locally in your browser and are not linked to your identity unless saved to an order.
  • Technical & Log Information: IP address, device operating system, browser type, geographic country location, and timestamp logs used strictly for site stability, caching, and fraud detection.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process personal information under the lawful bases of contract fulfillment, legal obligation, and legitimate business interests:

  • Fulfilling and dispatching your oversize denim shipments via DHL Express or FedEx.
  • Transmitting real-time tracking updates, delivery notifications, and receipt invoices.
  • Facilitating our 30-Day Baggy Guarantee exchange program.
  • Sending optional VIP Drop announcements (only when you explicitly opt-in to our newsletter).

4. Zero Data Monetization & Third-Party Sharing

OUR STRICT PLEDGE: WE NEVER SELL YOUR DATA

oversizejean.com has never sold, rented, or monetized customer personal data, and we never will. We do not participate in cross-context behavioral ad networks or data broker exchanges.

We share your data solely with vetted operational partners strictly necessary to complete your order:

  • Logistics Couriers: DHL Express, FedEx, Japan Post, USPS (only name, delivery destination, and tracking phone).
  • Payment Gateways: Stripe, Apple Pay, PayPal (tokenized, PCI-DSS Level 1 certified).
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Encrypted hosting providers with ISO 27001 certifications.

5. Payment Processing Security

All checkout transactions are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) with 256-bit encryption. oversizejean.com never sees, handles, or stores your raw credit card numbers or security CVVs on our servers. All financial transactions are tokenized directly through certified PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant processors.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use minimal cookies and HTML5 local storage strictly necessary for core shopping functions:

  • Cart & Wishlist State (`osj_cart`, `osj_wishlist`): Stores your selected products, sizes, and quantities in your local browser storage so your bag remains intact when you refresh.
  • Session Cookies: Maintains CSRF security tokens to prevent cross-site request forgery attacks.
  • No Invasive Advertising Trackers: We do not deploy third-party advertising pixels or fingerprinting scripts.

7. Data Retention Periods

We retain order history and transaction records for the duration required by applicable financial accounting and tax laws (typically 7 years for corporate audit compliance). Non-transactional inquiries and fit recommendations are discarded immediately after resolution.

8. Your GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Rights

Regardless of your jurisdiction, we extend global data control rights to all oversizejean.com users:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request an export of all personal data held concerning your account.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct inaccurate shipping or contact details at any time before dispatch.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request complete deletion of your customer profile, subject only to mandatory statutory tax retention requirements.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications with one click via the footer link in every email.
